# News stage

Reuses the existing XML parser, source snapshots and SQLite table. New built-in catalog: Fashion, Artificial Intelligence, Cryptocurrency, Finance, Technology. Existing custom source settings remain stored for compatibility; the main UI has no source JSON. Only the selected topic loads after explicit network consent. No startup/all-topic timer. Per-topic coalescing and four shared workers avoid duplicate downloads; manual refresh has a 30 s floor, ordinary refresh the saved interval. Switching topics cannot apply a late reply for another topic. Last successful data survives empty/error responses and restart.

RSS/Atom, encodings, entity/size limits, canonical URL dedup, publication IDs, bounded redirects and public-address checks are tested. Broad feeds require a title/section relevance match; known publication dates older than 30 days or >1 h in the future are excluded. Unknown dates are labelled unknown. No paywall/CAPTCHA bypass, full articles, filler or cross-topic fallback. Original card/skeleton/progress markup is retained, each article value copies separately.

Fresh production transport/source checks: all 17 endpoints returned HTTP 200 on 2026-11-13. `live-sources.json` includes headers, parser counts and publisher copyright/rights declarations inspected for this candidate. `news-live.json` records backend topic counts; `news-live-gui.json` separately records actual Electron card/link counts and screenshots. Counts are point-in-time observations, not a promise of future source availability.

Publisher usage review retained from the 2026-11-12 technical source audit and checked against fresh feed declarations: Guardian https://www.theguardian.com/help/feeds, WWD https://wwd.com/rss-feeds/, MarketWatch https://www.marketwatch.com/site/rss describe personal/non-commercial feed reading. WIRED https://www.wired.com/about/rss-feeds/ and CoinDesk https://www.coindesk.com/coindesk-news/2021/09/17/coindesk-rss document publisher feeds. Local headline/link reading includes source attribution and available author, opening the publisher's original article. This is not a licence for commercial redistribution. Full reuse terms for Vogue, TechCrunch, Ars, Verge, Engadget, BBC, Cointelegraph and Decrypt remain NOT_VERIFIED; live endpoint/parser/display checks are recorded separately. No publication is authorized or performed.
